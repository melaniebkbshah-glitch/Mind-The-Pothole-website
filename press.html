Short description

Mind the Pothole turns the familiar chaos of British roads into an old-school arcade driving challenge. Dodge potholes, roadworks and traffic, react to weather and road conditions, collect coins and push for higher scores across distinct city districts.

Key features

  • Top-down retro arcade driving
  • British road humour and personality
  • Potholes, roadworks, traffic lights and level crossings
  • Day, night and wet-weather conditions
  • Multiple vehicles and driver progression
  • Challenges, high scores and Steam achievements
