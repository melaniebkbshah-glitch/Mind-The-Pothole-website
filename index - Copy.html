MIND THE POTHOLE

Old-school arcade driving. Very British problems.

Mind the Pothole is a top-down arcade driving game built around the chaos, humour and hazards of British roads. Thread through traffic, react to changing conditions, avoid potholes and roadworks, and push for a better run each time.

Potholes & hazardsRead the road and react fast.
Traffic & crossingsRed lights, level crossings and busy streets.
Weather & districtsRain, night driving and distinct city areas.
Driver progressionVehicles, upgrades, challenges and high scores.
